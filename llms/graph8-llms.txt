# graph8

> graph8 is an autonomous revenue system that finds buyers, launches campaigns, runs conversations, and builds pipeline on one buyer graph.

## Platform

- [Platform overview](https://graph8.com/): How graph8 combines buyer data, signals, campaigns, conversations, and revenue workflows in one system.
- [Pricing](https://graph8.com/pricing/): Current plans for free buyer search, unlimited data, and usage-based revenue execution.
- [Data](https://graph8.com/platform/data/): Contact, company, intent, and buyer-graph data for building and operating a market.
- [Signals](https://graph8.com/platform/signals/): Website, intent, product, and market signals that identify when an account is ready for action.
- [Studio](https://graph8.com/platform/studio/): Business context, campaign strategy, content generation, account intelligence, and publishing in one workspace.
- [AI](https://graph8.com/platform/ai/): AI agents and governed workflows that operate against the same buyer graph and revenue memory.
- [Revenue](https://graph8.com/platform/revenue/): Pipeline, quotes, performance, and cash visibility connected to the work that created each outcome.
- [Data pipelines](https://graph8.com/platform/data-pipelines/): Event collection, transformation, destinations, replay, and warehouse connections.

## Key Facts

- graph8 joins data, signals, Studio, engagement, agents, pipeline, and revenue on one buyer graph.
- Public buyer search is available on the free plan; paid plans cover unlimited data and revenue execution.
- The Builder surface provides APIs, SDKs, MCP, sandbox access, and reusable UI components.
- CIENCE is the services layer and Tenbound is the research layer connected through the shared site navigation.
